式 (1)：论文要在前面加料的那台机器Eq. (1): the machine the paper feeds better ingredients

是什么WHAT

你在第 9 卡沙盒里按过的"平滑一步"，它的正式写法就是论文式 (1)。式 (1) 就干一件事：把每个顶点的特征，沿着它所在的超边做一次"组内平均"——人人向组员靠拢一步。它属于经典 HGNN（2019 年的老成果），不是论文的发明，但论文的一切改进都以它为底座。

The "smooth one step" button from card 9's sandbox has an official form: the paper's Eq. (1). Eq. (1) does one thing: it averages every vertex's feature with those of its hyperedge-mates — everyone takes one step toward the group. It belongs to the classic HGNN (a 2019 result), not to this paper — yet every improvement here stands on it.

先把"平均"的过程拆成三块积木（积木都懂了，公式就只剩符号翻译）：① 收集——每个顶点把自己的特征值交给自己所在的每条超边（收进一个"组篮"）；② 组内平均——每条超边的组篮里，把收来的值加起来、除以参与者人数（大超边里的单票不该因为人多而更响）；③ 归一化——每个顶点收下自己各条超边送回的"组平均值"后，整体除以 √(自己参加的超边条数)（人脉广的顶点不该因为票多而吵翻天）。

Split the "averaging" into three building blocks (once you have the blocks, the equation is just symbol translation): (1) Collect — every vertex hands its value to each hyperedge it belongs to (into a per-edge basket); (2) Averaging within edge — each basket sums its values and divides by how many members contributed (a lone vote in a big edge must not shout louder for the crowd); (3) Normalize — each vertex collects the group averages sent back and divides the total by √(how many hyperedges it joined) (well-connected vertices must not drown everyone with extra votes).

三块积木拼起来，就是式 (1)。读它时把符号按下面的表翻译即可——不需要自己会算矩阵乘法，例 1 会把每一步替你算完。

Stack the three blocks and you have Eq. (1). Read it by translating symbols through the table below — you never need to run matrix multiplication yourself; Example 1 does every step for you.

读法（从右往左）：Xl 是第 l 层的特征表 → 乘 Θ（机器自己学的旋钮）→ 经过归一化与收集的连乘（= 上面三块积木）→ 最后套 σ（把数值折一下，增加表达力的"弯折"）。别被连乘吓到：它按顺序执行，就是"收集→平均→归一化"。Reading (right to left): Xl is the layer-l feature table → times Θ (the machine's learned knobs) → through the chain of collect-and-average matrices (= the three blocks above) → wrapped in σ (a bend that adds expressiveness). Do not fear the chain: executed in order, it is simply "collect → average → normalize".

例子EXAMPLE

例 1（最小算例，全部手算，跟着走就行）：2 个顶点、1 条超边 {1,2}：编组表 H 告诉计算机"1 在组里、2 也在组里"；特征 x₁ = 2、x₂ = 4；权重全 1；旋钮 Θ = 1（先看纯平均，σ 也先当"原样返回"）。走三块积木：①收集：组篮收到 2 和 4；②平均：两个数相加得 6，除以成员数 2 → 3；③归一化：每个顶点只参加这 1 条超边，√1 = 1，除不除都一样。结果：两个顶点都变成 3.0——恰好是 (2+4)÷2。这就是"平滑一步"的全部。

Example 1 (minimal, fully hand-computed — just follow along): 2 vertices, 1 hyperedge {1,2}: the roster H says "1 is in, 2 is in"; features x₁ = 2, x₂ = 4; weights all 1; knob Θ = 1 (watch pure averaging; σ also behaves as "return as-is"). The three blocks: (1) collect: the basket receives 2 and 4; (2) average: add → 6, divide by 2 members → 3; (3) normalize: each vertex joined this one edge, √1 = 1, dividing changes nothing. Result: both vertices become 3.0 — exactly (2+4)÷2. That is the whole of "one smoothing step".

例 2（对上沙盒，看清 √ 的作用）：第 9 卡沙盒的 4 顶点链上，[10,0,0,0] 按一次得到 [5.00, 3.54, 0, 0]。用积木解释：v1 的组篮收到 10 和 0 → 平均 5.00（v1 只在这 1 条超边里，归一化无效果）；v2 在两个组里，它从 e1 的分红是 5，但 v2 参加 2 条超边 → 5 ÷ √2 ≈ 3.54。那个当年"莫名"的 3.54，就是积木 ③ 的除法：干得多的顶点，每一票都按 √票数 打折。

Example 2 (reconciling with the sandbox — see √ at work): on card 9's 4-vertex chain, [10,0,0,0] once yields [5.00, 3.54, 0, 0]. In block terms: v1's basket receives 10 and 0 → average 5.00 (v1 sits in this one edge only, so normalizing changes nothing); v2 belongs to two edges — its dividend from e1 is 5, but v2 joined 2 hyperedges → 5 ÷ √2 ≈ 3.54. The once-"mysterious" 3.54 is just block (3)'s division: a busier vertex gets every vote discounted by √(its vote count).

记住这台机器的两个软肋（第 17 卡已点名，此处再次强调）：Two soft spots to remember (named in card 17, re-emphasized here): ① 每过一层 = 一次平均——层数一多就趋向"所有顶点一个样"（过平滑；实验篇 Table VIII 会给出实测崩塌曲线）；② 积木 ③ 的"除以 √参加条数"已经改写了"参加几条超边"这个原始计数——结构细节从这里开始失真。论文的两台备料机器，正是冲着这两点来的。 (1) Each layer = one averaging round — many layers drift toward "everyone identical" (over-smoothing; Table VIII in the experiments gives the measured collapse); (2) block (3)'s "divide by √count" already rewrites the raw "how many hyperedges" number — structural detail begins to distort right here. The paper's two prep machines aim exactly at these two.
