两个型号摆在货架上，拿哪个？Two models on the shelf — which one?

是什么WHAT

方法篇的最后一个问题：第 34 卡的两个型号（RD = Rule-Driven，只吃规则账；DD = Dual-Driven，规则账 + 结构账都吃），到底什么场景拿哪个？论文给了判断口诀——看你数据里的"结构有多重"。

The method part's last question: card 34's two models (RD = Rule-Driven, eats the rule ledger only; DD = Dual-Driven, eats rules plus structure) — which one for which setting? The paper hands down a rule of thumb: gauge how heavy the structure is in your data.

先把"结构重不重"说清：用超边（一条边圈住一群人的边）表达的群体关系越多、圈得越大，结构就越"重"；反过来，关系基本是"一对一、两两成对"的（普通图），结构就"轻"——那时结构账里其实没多少信息可喂。

"Heavy structure" in plain terms: the more relations live as hyperedges (one edge embracing a whole crowd) and the bigger the crowds, the heavier the structure; when relations are essentially one-to-one pairs (an ordinary graph), structure is light — the structural ledger has little to feed.

选型口诀：结构越"抱团"，DD 越占优；关系越"两两成对"，RD 越占优。下一段的实测数字给这条口诀压上证据。

The rule in one line: the more the structure clusters into crowds, the more DD pays; the more relations stay pairwise, the more RD pays. The measured numbers below back this rule.

例子EXAMPLE

例 1（决策表，两个真实数字）：Table V 给的是十数据集上的分类准确率（单位 %，越大越好）；比较口径是"DD 减 RD"。图数据集 Cora：RD 88.64、DD 87.22 → 差 −1.42（Cora 的每条关系恰好 2 个端点，平均边度 = 2，属于"两两成对"）→ 拿 RD。超图数据集 CC-Cora：DD 76.06、RD 74.30 → 差 +1.76（关系成"团"出现）→ 拿 DD。选型不是拍脑袋，是一条能落表的规则。

Example 1 (a decision table, two real numbers): Table V reports classification accuracy across ten datasets (unit %, higher is better); the comparison convention is "DD minus RD". Graph dataset Cora: RD 88.64, DD 87.22 → gap −1.42 (Cora's relations hold exactly two endpoints — average edge degree 2, "pairwise") → take RD. Hypergraph CC-Cora: DD 76.06, RD 74.30 → gap +1.76 (relations come in crowds) → take DD. Selection is not a hunch but a rule that fits on a slipsheet.

例 2（边界情形，如实走查）：Github（图）：RD 86.20、DD 86.00，差 −0.20——但论文自己的重复实验波动就有 ±0.16 / ±0.33（Table V 的 ± 号，五次不同随机种子的波动范围）。Tencent（超图）：DD 95.83、RD 95.65，差 +0.18，而波动达 ±1.65。读法：当数据形态处在中间地带、差距落进波动里时，口诀给不了确定答案——这也解释了论文为什么用"平均排名 DD 1.5、RD 1.7"而不是"DD 全面胜出"来汇报。

Example 2 (a boundary case, honestly walked): Github (graph): RD 86.20, DD 86.00, gap −0.20 — yet the paper's own re-runs fluctuate by ±0.16 / ±0.33 (the ± figures in Table V: five random seeds). Tencent (hypergraph): DD 95.83 vs RD 95.65, gap +0.18, with fluctuations up to ±1.65. Reading: when the data's shape is middling and the gap drowns in variability, the rule cannot decide — which is why the paper reports average ranks (DD 1.5, RD 1.7) rather than a clean sweep.
