是什么WHAT

图 2 是学究的"记账流程图"：从左边一队训练好的树出发，中间两条流水线分别记录每条规则的内容和位置，右边订成一本"知识嵌入特征"。它回答的问题是：上上卡说的"内容 + 位置"两条记录，具体长什么样、怎么装订。

Figure 2 is the scholar's bookkeeping flowchart: from a team of trained trees on the left, two middle lines record each rule's content and position, bound on the right into "knowledge-embedded features". It answers: what do those two records (content + position) look like in practice, and how are they bound?

先认四个隔间：最左是"训练台"（Features → Gradient Boosting Decision Tree → Task 色点：树队在这里训练出来）；中上是"内容登记处"（标题 Rule Content Mapping μc：两棵示例树 T₁、T₂ 和它们的提问点）；中下是"位置登记处"（标题 Rule Position Mapping μp：一条随深度下降的曲线和三个大小不同的点）；最右是"装订成品"（标题 Knowledge-Embedded Features：两摞方块，标着 1st Layer、2nd Layer）。

Four compartments first: far left the "training bench" (Features → Gradient Boosting Decision Tree → Task dots: the team is trained here); upper middle the "content desk" (labeled Rule Content Mapping μc: the two sample trees T₁, T₂ and their question nodes); lower middle the "position desk" (Rule Position Mapping μp: a curve sloping down with depth, plus three dots of different sizes); far right the "bound product" (Knowledge-Embedded Features: two stacks labeled 1st Layer, 2nd Layer).

一行文字版（图若显示不清，照这行读）：特征 → 树队（T₁、T₂）→ 提问点标上问题内容（μc 面版）→ 同一条规则再按"第几层"配一个权重（μp 面版）→ 两路记录在右侧叠成两摞 → 知识嵌入特征到手。

One text line (if the image is unclear, read this): features → tree team (T₁, T₂) → question nodes labeled with their questions (the μc panel) → each rule weighted by "which layer" (the μp panel) → the two records stack into two piles at right → knowledge-embedded features delivered.

常见误解：Common misconception: 图上左面板画着 "Task" 色点，容易让人以为树是拿来输出任务预测的——恰恰相反：训练台产出的树，其预测在这里没有后续；被装订的只有提问点。图里 "Task" 只是说明"树是照这个任务训出来的"。 The left panel's "Task" dots may suggest the trees output task predictions — the opposite holds: the bench's tree predictions have no downstream here; only question nodes get bound. "Task" merely notes "the trees were trained for this task".
例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（训练台 → 内容登记处）：左上：特征表进 Gradient Boosting Decision Tree 方块（第 12 卡的接力树队就在这里训练），旁生一个"Task"输出——提醒任务是什么。箭头从中缝分两路上行，进入 μc 面版：那里画着 T₁、T₂ 两棵树，每个提问点旁边标着问题原文（"x₂ ≥ 2"、"x₁ ≥ 1"、"x₃ ≥ 5"、"x₁ ≥ 2"、"x₂ ≥ 1"），底部一排 A / B / C 叶子。为什么这格重要：提问点的问题原文就是"规则内容"——μc 记录的是它。

Walk 1 (bench → content desk): upper-left: the feature table enters the Gradient Boosting Decision Tree box (cards 12–13's relay team trains right here), with a "Task" output beside it noting the objective. Arrows split upward through the seam into the μc panel: two trees T₁, T₂, each question node tagged with its question ("x₂ ≥ 2", "x₁ ≥ 1", "x₃ ≥ 5", "x₁ ≥ 2", "x₂ ≥ 1"), and a row of A / B / C leaves at bottom. Why this cell matters: the question wording is the rule's content — that is what μc records.

走查 2（位置登记处）：μp 面版画着一条从左上到右下缓降的曲线（横轴 = 深度），以及三个半径依次变小的点，标着 d(o) = 1、2、3。曲线和点讲的是同一件事：越往深层，规则分到的权重越小（为什么？纠错接力里先问的问题摘走最大的误差，第 13、27 卡）。点的大小 = 权重的大小，视觉上就一句话："根附近的规则分量重"。

Walk 2 (position desk): the μp panel shows a gently descending curve (horizontal axis = depth) and three dots shrinking in radius, labeled d(o) = 1, 2, 3. Curve and dots say one thing: the deeper the layer, the smaller the weight a rule receives (why? the relay's earlier questions grabbed the biggest residual — cards 13, 27). Dot size = weight size, visually just: "root-side rules carry more".

走查 3（装订成品）：左右两路箭头汇入最右面的 Knowledge-Embedded Features：两摞竖方块，分别标 1st Layer、2nd Layer——表示"多条记录叠起来"，一摞对着一棵树收来的那批规则。每格方块就是一个数（= 内容记录 × 位置权重，第 31 卡逐格算）。为什么这格重要：它把"5 条规则"变成"每个顶点 5 个数的向量"，从此可以像特征一样被送进下游。

Walk 3 (bound product): the two arrow streams merge into the far-right Knowledge-Embedded Features: two stacks of bars labeled 1st Layer, 2nd Layer — "records stacked", one stack per tree's rule batch. Each bar is one number (= content record × position weight; computed bar by bar at card 31). Why it matters: it turns "5 rules" into "a 5-number vector per vertex", ready to travel downstream like any feature.
