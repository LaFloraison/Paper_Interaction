表 I 是实验的"样本池清单"：十个数据集的规模统计——顶点数、边/超边数、特征数、两类平均度、类别数；后面所有结果表都在这十份"考题"上评分。四份是普通图（Cora、Github、Amazon-P、Amazon-C），六份是真超图（CA-Cora、DBLP-Conf、CC-Cora、Tencent、ModelNet40、Heloc）。

Table I is the sample pool: size statistics of ten datasets — vertices, edges/hyperedges, features, two average degrees, classes; every later results table scores on these ten "exam papers". Four are ordinary graphs (Cora, Github, Amazon-P, Amazon-C); six are true hypergraphs (CA-Cora, DBLP-Conf, CC-Cora, Tencent, ModelNet40, Heloc).

读表前先分清两个度数——它们方向不同：平均边度（Averaged Degree of Edges）= 平均每条关系牵涉几个点（两两图的这一列恒为 2；超图可大可小）；平均顶点度（Averaged Degrees of Vertices）= 平均每个点牵涉几条关系。

Before reading, split the two degrees — they point different ways: average edge degree = how many vertices one relation involves on average (always 2 for pairwise graphs; anything for hypergraphs); average vertex degree = how many relations one vertex touches on average.

例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（读一行）：Cora 行逐格念——2,708 篇论文（顶点）；7,440 条引用关系（边）；1,433 个词（特征，每篇论文的词袋表示）；平均边度 2（引用关系两两发生）；平均顶点度 3.9（平均每篇被几条/发出几条引用）；7 个类别（待分类的主题数）。这一行就是"七分类任务、2,708 个样本"的完整简历。

Walk 1 (read one row): Cora row by cell — 2,708 papers (vertices); 7,440 citation relations (edges); 1,433 words (features, each paper as a bag of words); average edge degree 2 (citations are pairwise); average vertex degree 3.9; 7 classes. This row is the full CV of a "7-way classification task with 2,708 samples".

走查 2（三行对照，看懂"图 vs 超图"）：同一批论文两种画法的 Cora（7,440 条两两边、平均边度 2）与 CA-Cora（970 条超边、平均边度 4.5）——后者用少得多的关系装下同样的团结构。再看极端的 DBLP-Conf：仅 20 条超边却圈住 4,057 位作者，平均一条超边 982.2 人——自检：20 × 982.2 = 19,644 人次，摊给 4,057 人 ≈ 4.84，与表里的"平均顶点度 4.8"吻合（超图两列互相印证）。

Walk 2 (three rows, grasping graph-vs-hypergraph): the same corpus two ways — Cora (7,440 pairwise edges, avg edge degree 2) and CA-Cora (970 hyperedges, avg edge degree 4.5): far fewer relations carry the same crowds. Then the extreme, DBLP-Conf: just 20 hyperedges embracing 4,057 authors, each averaging 982.2 people — self-check: 20 × 982.2 = 19,644 memberships over 4,057 authors ≈ 4.84, matching the table's vertex degree 4.8 (the hypergraph's two columns cross-confirm).
