式 (6)：越靠根的规则，分量越重Eq. (6): rules near the root carry more weight

是什么WHAT

内容登记完成了；位置登记处的活是给每条规则配一个分量。规律很朴素：纠错接力里先问的问题摘走最大的误差（第 13 卡），所以越靠根（层号越小）的规则，分量越大。

Content is settled; the position desk's job is giving each rule a weight. The pattern is plain: in the relay, earlier questions grabbed the biggest residual (card 13), so rules nearer the root (smaller layer number) weigh more.

位置登记表的写法：μp(o) = α ÷ (d(o) + β)，其中 d(o) 是这条规则的层号（根 = 0），α、β 是两个可调旋钮。层号在分母上——层号越大（越深），除出来的数越小，分量自然越轻。

The position desk's formula: μp(o) = α ÷ (d(o) + β), where d(o) is the rule's layer number (root = 0) and α, β are two tunable knobs. The layer sits in the denominator — a larger (deeper) layer divides into a smaller number, so weight falls naturally.

逐符号：d(o) = 层号（根 0、往下加 1；第 25 卡登场的老熟人）；α = 总幅度旋钮（整体抬高或压低这条曲线）；β = 缓冲旋钮（给分母垫底，防止层号 0 时除爆，也决定衰减的快慢）。论文把 α、β 用"网格搜索"在 {6, 7, 8, 9, 10} 里逐个数据集试出（Table IV 会看到每行各不同）。计算只需除法，例 1 全包。Symbol by symbol: d(o) = layer number (root 0, +1 downward; our friend from card 25); α = amplitude knob (raises or lowers the whole curve); β = buffer knob (keeps the denominator from blowing up at layer 0 and sets how fast the decay falls). The paper grid-searches α, β over {6, 7, 8, 9, 10} per dataset (Table IV shows each row differing). The math is one division; Example 1 covers it.

例子EXAMPLE

例 1（CC-Cora 的真实参数，逐层算）：论文给 CC-Cora 选了 α = 9、β = 8。层号 0 到 3 逐个代：μp(0) = 9 ÷ (0+8) = 1.125；μp(1) = 9 ÷ 9 = 1.000；μp(2) = 9 ÷ 10 = 0.900；μp(3) = 9 ÷ 11 ≈ 0.818。读这张小表：从根到第 3 层，分量从 1.125 缓降到 0.818——根上的规则分量最重，且衰减是温和平滑的（不是一刀切）。

Example 1 (CC-Cora's real parameters, layer by layer): the paper picked α = 9, β = 8 for CC-Cora. Substitute layers 0–3: μp(0) = 9 ÷ (0+8) = 1.125; μp(1) = 9 ÷ 9 = 1.000; μp(2) = 9 ÷ 10 = 0.900; μp(3) = 9 ÷ 11 ≈ 0.818. Read the little table: from root to layer 3 the weight eases 1.125 → 0.818 — root rules weigh most, and the decay is gentle, not a cliff.

例 2（换一组旋钮，曲线就换一张脸）：Amazon-P 的数据用 α = 6、β = 6：μp(0) = 6 ÷ 6 = 1.0；μp(1) = 6 ÷ 7 ≈ 0.857；μp(2) = 6 ÷ 8 = 0.75。对比例 1：曲线形状都是"缓降"，但同一层号下的分量不同（0.9 vs 0.75）——这就是"每数据集网格搜索 α、β"的意义：让衰减速度适配各数据集的树形。

Example 2 (swap the knobs, the curve changes face): Amazon-P uses α = 6, β = 6: μp(0) = 6 ÷ 6 = 1.0; μp(1) = 6 ÷ 7 ≈ 0.857; μp(2) = 6 ÷ 8 = 0.75. Compare with Example 1: both curves ease gently, but the same layer carries different weight (0.9 vs 0.75) — that is what per-dataset grid search buys: decay tuned to each dataset's tree shapes.
