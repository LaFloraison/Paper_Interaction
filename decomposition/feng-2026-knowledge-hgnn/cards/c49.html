读完论文，你最该接着问的六件事Six things to ask next

上一卡算的是"数字对不对"；本卡问的是"论文没说的部分"——这些问题不是挑刺，而是你把方法真正用起来时会撞上的墙。

The last card checked the figures; this one asks what the paper never says — not nitpicking, but the walls you will hit when you actually deploy it.

##问题Question为什么值得追Why it matters
1GBDT 的"预定义任务"从哪来？Where does the GBDT's "predefined task" come from?论文只说 TDR 在"预定义任务"上预训练，但没说这任务和最终分类任务什么关系。若它就是最终任务本身，等于用（可能含测试集的）标签训练规则矿——需要论文明确"预训练只用训练集标签"才能排除疑虑。原文未交代。The TDR pre-trains on a "predefined task", yet the paper never says how that task relates to the final one. If it is the final task, the rule mine trains on labels (possibly including tests) — only an explicit "pre-training sees training labels only" would settle it. The paper stays silent.
2μc 的二值化丢了什么？What does μc's binarization discard?"满足记 0、不满足记 1"把连续差距压成一位：x₂ = 2.01 与 x₂ = 5.0 对规则"x₂ ≥ 2"同样记 0——离阈值多远的信息全没了。论文没有做"软化版 μc"的对照实验，无法知道这一压缩损失多大。"Satisfied → 0, missed → 1" squashes continuous distance into one bit: x₂ = 2.01 and x₂ = 5.0 both record 0 against "x₂ ≥ 2" — all "how far from the threshold" information vanishes. No softened-μc baseline appears, so the size of that loss is unknown.
3每换一个数据集都要重训一整支树队——代价多大？Every new dataset retrains a whole tree team — at what cost?表 IV 的树数从 20 到 190，GBDT 预训练是每次实验的固定开销；论文没有报告任何耗时（只说了机器配置），也没有讨论"预训练一次、多任务复用"的可能。Table IV's tree counts run 20–190; GBDT pre-training is fixed overhead per run, yet the paper reports no timings (only the hardware) and never discusses "train once, reuse across tasks".
4"超图"从哪来，谁来保证它是对的？Where do the hyperedges come from, and who checks them?图数据集要用 KNN 在特征空间造超边（第 37 卡），k 取 5–20 全靠经验；Heloc/ModelNet40 的超图更是为本文现造。换一个 k，结论还稳吗？论文没有 k 的敏感性实验。Graph datasets get KNN-built hyperedges (card 37) with k chosen by rule of thumb (5–20); Heloc/ModelNet40's hypergraphs are purpose-built here. Would another k move the conclusions? No k-sensitivity study appears.
5规则账会不会越用越长、撑爆维度？Can the rule ledger grow until dimensions explode?Z 的维度 = 2 × 各树提问点数之和（第 32/33 卡）。树数 190、深度 7（表 IV 的 Github）时，维度可以极大；论文没有报告各数据集的 Cr 实际取值，也没讨论维度控制（剪枝、共享规则）。Z's width = 2 × the summed question-node count (cards 32/33). With 190 trees at depth 7 (Table IV's Github) it can balloon; the paper reports neither the actual Cr per dataset nor any width control (pruning, shared rules).
6Github 上为什么赢不了？Why can't it win on Github?表 VI 里 Github 的三行本文型号都没能超过 HGNN（Production Δ = −0.09，归纳行 Δ 为负）。论文承认"图数据集上 RD 略胜"的规律，但对 Github 这个例外没有个案分析——是数据太简单、还是超边构造没造好？读者无从判断。On Github none of the three paper models beats HGNN (Production Δ = −0.09; inductive Δ negative). The paper names the "RD edges out on graph data" trend but offers no case analysis for Github — too-simple data, or badly built hyperedges? The reader cannot tell.
把追问变成研究课题：Turning questions into research: 其中第 2 条（μc 软化）与第 4 条（k 敏感性）是现成的、一两周可做的消融实验；第 1 条（预训练任务的泄漏边界）则是复现这篇论文时必须先向作者问清楚的一条。读到好论文的尽头，就是能开出这样的清单。 Items 2 (softened μc) and 4 (k-sensitivity) are ready-made ablations doable in a week or two; item 1 (the pre-training leakage boundary) is something you must ask the authors before reproducing. Reaching the end of a good paper means holding exactly such a list.
