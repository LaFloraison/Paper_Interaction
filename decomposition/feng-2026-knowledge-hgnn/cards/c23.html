是什么WHAT

图 1 是一张"工厂平面图"：左边两个车间负责备料，中缝把两份料订在一起，右边拿订好的料顺着关系网打分。它不证明某个数字，而是回答一个问题：备料车间做出来的东西，是怎样一毫米不差地接进打分机器的？

Figure 1 is a factory floor plan: two workshops on the left prepare ingredients, the mid-seam staples them together, and the right side scores along the relational network. It proves no number; it answers one question: how do the prep workshops' products plug into the scoring machine without a millimeter of slack?

先补一个词的底：超图 = 一种关系图，它的一条"边"可以同时连两个以上的点（普通图的边只连两个）——所以它天然能表达"一群人共同做一件事"（读书会、同班、同一次团购）。图里那张彩色小图就是超图的样子。

One word to ground first: a hypergraph = a relation graph whose "edge" may join more than two vertices at once (a plain graph's edge joins exactly two) — so it naturally expresses "a group doing one thing" (a reading club, a class, one bulk order). The little colorful drawing in the figure is such a hypergraph.

三块地界，一句话各说清它们是干什么的：左车间（论文缩写 KE-Phase）= 备料车间，两条并排的流水线各做一份料；中缝（论文缩写 MDK-Fusion）= 装订台，把两份料订成一份；右车间（论文缩写 iHGNN）= 加工车间，那台打分机器就在这里，顺着超图关系出分类结果。

Three territories, one line each on what they do: left workshop (paper's abbreviation KE-Phase) = the prep floor, with two parallel lines each producing one sheet; mid-seam (paper's abbreviation MDK-Fusion) = the binding desk, stapling the two sheets into one; right workshop (paper's abbreviation iHGNN) = the processing floor, where the scoring machine sits and emits classifications along hypergraph relations.

先记住这张图的一行文字版（图若显示不清，照这行读）：彩色超图 → 0/1 编组表 → φ、Σ 两个小算子 → 绿色"结构料" ｜ 特征表 → 决策树 → 规则-特征映射 → 蓝色"规则料" ｜（两份料：先 ⊙ 相乘、再 ▷ 拼接）→ 紫色"知识特征" → HGNN → 三个预测色点。

One line of pure text to remember the figure by (if the image is unclear, read this): colorful hypergraph → 0/1 roster table → two little operators φ, Σ → green "structure sheet" | feature table → decision tree → rule-feature map → blue "rule sheet" | (the two sheets: first ⊙ multiply, then ▷ concatenate) → purple "knowledge features" → HGNN → three prediction dots.

论文图 1 原图（Feng et al., TPAMI 2026）。底部图例两个符号：⊙ 与 ▷。Original Figure 1 (Feng et al., TPAMI 2026). Bottom legend, two symbols: ⊙ and ▷.
常见误解：Common misconception: 左车间的两条流水线是上下并排、同时开工的两条支路（上面管结构、下面管规则），不是"先跑上面再跑下面"的一条串行线。图上它们上下并列，正是因为互不依赖。 The two left-workshop lines are stacked and run in parallel (top handles structure, bottom handles rules), not one serial line "top first, bottom after". They sit side by side precisely because neither depends on the other.
例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（上支路，四格）：① 左上角的彩色超图小图 = 原始数据的样子；② 它的 0/1 编组表（电脑能算的形式：每行一个点、每列一个组，属于就填 1）；③ 箭头进入 φ 与 Σ 两个符号——把这张表"按行加起来再折一下"的两个固定小算子（第 25 卡逐字讲，这里只需知道它们把表压成一串数字）；④ 产出绿色小方块，标签 Structure-Related Embedding（结构相关嵌入）。为什么这四格重要：它们保证"结构知识"被显式记下来，而不是像老方法那样在平均中丢失。

Walk 1 (upper branch, four boxes): (1) the colorful hypergraph top-left = the raw data; (2) its 0/1 roster table (the computer-friendly form: one row per vertex, one column per group, a 1 marks membership); (3) the arrow enters two little operators φ and Σ — fixed procedures that "sum each row, then bend it" (word-for-word at card 25; here just know they squash the table into a string of numbers); (4) out comes the green sliver labeled Structure-Related Embedding. Why these four boxes matter: they guarantee structural knowledge gets written down explicitly, instead of vanishing into averaging like the old method.

走查 2（下支路，四格）：① 左下角的特征表（每个点一串描述它的数字）；② 一棵预训练好的决策树示意图——树上标着 d(o) = 1、2、3，那是"第几层问题"的编号（根在第一层）；③ 规则-特征映射的示意，旁边写着 μp(o) = α/(d(o)+β)——位置越靠上层的问题，给的权重越大（第 30 卡逐符号讲）；④ 产出蓝色方块，标签 Task-Related Embedding（任务相关嵌入）。树右边那排 "⟌" 记号表示：每个提问点都会贡献一个数字位。

Walk 2 (lower branch, four boxes): (1) the feature table lower-left (a string of numbers describing each vertex); (2) a pre-trained decision tree sketch — depths d(o) = 1, 2, 3 mark "which layer of questions" (root sits at layer one); (3) the rule-feature map sketch bearing μp(o) = α/(d(o)+β) — shallower questions get heavier weights (disassembled at card 30); (4) out comes the blue block labeled Task-Related Embedding. The "⟌" marks at the tree's right signal: every question node contributes one number slot.

走查 3（装订台与加工车间）：两份料先过 ⊙（读作"逐位相乘"：把绿色的一个数按位乘进蓝色的每个数里——像给整张成绩单统一打个折）；再与蓝色原稿一起过 ▷（拼接：把打折版和原版并排订成一页）。产出的紫色"知识特征"进入右车间，沿超图反复平均后，输出预测色点。（第 33 卡的式 (9) 正是这两步的正式写法。）

Walk 3 (binding desk and processing floor): the two sheets first pass ⊙ ("per-slot multiply": the green scalar multiplies into every blue number — like applying one discount factor to a whole report card); then join the blue original through ▷ (concatenation: the discounted version and the original are stapled side by side). The purple "knowledge features" enter the right workshop, average along the hypergraph, and emit prediction dots. (Eq. (9) at card 33 is the formal writing of exactly these two moves.)
