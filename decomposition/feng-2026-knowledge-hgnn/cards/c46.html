表 IX：换掉打分机器，知识账照样管用Table IX: swap the scoring machine — the ledgers still pay

是什么WHAT

论文自称"通用框架"（第 21 卡）：知识嵌入是可以插在任何超图网络前面的前置工序。表 IX 就是验收：把打分机器换成六种不同的超图网络——GCN（把超图退化当图算）、HGNN（论文的默认底座）、HGNN+（增强版 HGNN）、HNHN（超图-超边双通道）、LightHGNN（轻量蒸馏版）、AllSetTransformer（基于集合注意力）——每种都跑三行（Production / Transductive / Inductive）。

The paper calls itself a general framework (card 21): knowledge embedding is a prep stage that plugs ahead of any hypergraph network. Table IX is the acceptance test: six different scoring machines — GCN (a hypergraph flattened to a graph), HGNN (the paper's default), HGNN+ (enhanced HGNN), HNHN (a vertex-edge dual channel model), LightHGNN (a lightweight distilled one), AllSetTransformer (set attention) — each run in three settings (Production / Transductive / Inductive).

读法：每个模型块里，Origin 列 = 该模型原装成绩（不插知识账），RD-HGNN / DD-HGNN 列 = 插上知识账之后的成绩，最后一列 Δ = 两者中较好者 − Origin。整张表只问一个问题：Δ 是正还是负？

How to read: in each block, Origin = the model as shipped (no knowledge ledgers), RD-HGNN / DD-HGNN = the same model with ledgers plugged in, and Δ = the better of the two minus Origin. The whole table asks one question: is Δ positive or negative?

例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（扫描 Δ 列：18 个格子有无例外）：六模型 × 三设定 = 18 个 Δ 值，逐格看符号——18 格全为正，一个例外都没有（全表最小增益约 +1.09、最大约 +8.35）。换句话说：无论把知识账插到哪台机器前面，成绩都不降、只升。具体每格多少，习题会让你亲手取两格来验。

Walk 1 (scan the Δ columns: any exceptions among 18 cells?): six models × three settings = 18 Δ values, sign by sign — all 18 positive, not one exception (the table's smallest gain ≈ +1.09, largest ≈ +8.35). In other words: plug the ledgers ahead of any machine and scores never drop, only rise. The exercises will have you pull two cells and verify by hand.

走查 2（谁受益最大，谁"扶不起"）：Δ 最大的一族是 LightHGNN：+5.80 / +5.16 / +8.35——复算一例：其归纳行 Origin 65.46、插账后最好 73.82，73.82 − 65.46 = 8.35 ✓。为什么偏偏是"轻量版"受益最多？回到第 24/32 卡的道理：LightHGNN 靠蒸馏把大模型压小，它被压掉的信息恰恰包括结构细节与任务规则——而知识账正是这两样的"备份"，插回去等于把蒸馏时丢掉的部分捞回来。反面是 HGNN 的 Production 行增益最小（+2.35）（它本来就是这套系里结构保留较好的一款），说明"提升空间取决于底座缺什么"。

Walk 2 (who gains most — and who has little to gain): the biggest payer is LightHGNN: +5.80 / +5.16 / +8.35 — recompute one: inductive Origin 65.46, plugged-in best 73.82: 73.82 − 65.46 = 8.35 ✓. Why the lightweight one? Cards 24/32: LightHGNN is distilled small, and what distillation squeezed out includes exactly structural detail and task rules — the ledgers are backups of both, so plugging them in recovers the loss. The other extreme: HGNN's Production row gains least (+2.35) (already structure-preserving), showing that headroom depends on what a backbone lacks.
