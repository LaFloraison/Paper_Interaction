是什么WHAT

表 V 是全篇最重的一张表：七个方法（列）× 十个数据集（行）的分类准确率（%），每格写成"均值 ± 波动"，最后一行是平均排名——数字越小越好。方法分四族：纯特征派（MLP、GBDT）、结构派（GCN、HGNN）、树网混合（TLF）、本文（RD-HGNN、DD-HGNN）。

Table V is the paper's heaviest table: seven methods (columns) × ten datasets (rows) of classification accuracy (%), each cell "mean ± spread", with average ranks in the last row — lower is better. The methods fall into four camps: feature-only (MLP, GBDT), structure-only (GCN, HGNN), tree-net hybrid (TLF), and this paper (RD-HGNN, DD-HGNN).

三个读表工具先备好：① ± 后面的数是五次不同随机种子的波动（第 20 卡的规矩）——两方法之差若小于波动，就不该当真；② 加粗/下划线标出该行最优与次优；③ 平均排名把十行的相对位置折成一个数，防止"只挑最好的一行说事"。

Three reading tools first: (1) the number after ± is the spread over five random seeds (card 20's rule) — a gap smaller than the spread should not be taken seriously; (2) bold/underline mark each row's best and runner-up; (3) average rank folds ten relative positions into one number, preventing cherry-picking.

走查 1（读一行的冠军与差距）：Cora 行七格：MLP 69.04、GCN 83.63、GBDT 74.30、HGNN 81.81、TLF 65.11、RD 88.64、DD 87.22。冠军 RD 比最强的结构派 GCN 高 88.64 − 83.63 = 5.01 个百分点；比同族基础 HGNN 也高出一截（差多少，稍后习题请你亲手算）。读数时顺带看波动：RD 的 ±1.34 远小于这 5 个点的差距——这个优势不是运气。

Walk 1 (read one row's champion and margin): Cora's seven cells: MLP 69.04, GCN 83.63, GBDT 74.30, HGNN 81.81, TLF 65.11, RD 88.64, DD 87.22. Champion RD beats the strongest structure-only GCN by 88.64 − 83.63 = 5.01 points, and its own base HGNN by a margin the exercises will have you compute. Note the spreads while at it: RD's ±1.34 is far below a 5-point margin — this advantage is not luck.

走查 2（读最后一行 + 复算两个"论文声称"）：平均排名：MLP 5.8、GCN 3.9、GBDT 5.1、HGNN 3.3、TLF 6.1、RD 1.7、DD 1.5——本文两个型号分列第一、第二。我们复算了这张排名表：RD 十行名次之和 = 17（均值 1.7 ✓）、DD = 15（1.5 ✓）。
再验两个正文声称：①"平均增益 5.86"——复算 DD 列十行均值 87.648 减 GBDT 均值 81.792 = 5.86 ✓（口径 = DD 对 GBDT）。②"平均排名 1.9 和 1.7"——表格最后一行明明白白写着 1.7 和 1.5；正文这两个数与自己的表对不上（第 48 批判卡记账）。

Walk 2 (read the last row + recompute two paper claims): average ranks: MLP 5.8, GCN 3.9, GBDT 5.1, HGNN 3.3, TLF 6.1, RD 1.7, DD 1.5 — the paper's two models place first and second. We recomputed this rank table: RD's ten ranks sum to 17 (mean 1.7 ✓), DD to 15 (1.5 ✓).
Then two text claims checked: (1) "average gain 5.86" — DD's column mean 87.648 minus GBDT's 81.792 = 5.86 ✓ (DD-versus-GBDT convention). (2) "average ranks 1.9 and 1.7" — the table's last row plainly reads 1.7 and 1.5; the text disagrees with its own table (card 48 keeps the account).
