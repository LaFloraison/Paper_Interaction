图 3 是三个并排的"敏感度实验"：左格拧 α、中格拧 β、右格往特征里掺噪声，各画一条准确率响应曲线。它们回答同一类问题：把某个因素从很小调到很大，成绩会怎么动？三个子图的横轴分别是 α（1→10）、β（1→10）、噪声比例（0→80%）。

Figure 3 is three side-by-side sensitivity experiments: left twists α, middle twists β, right pours noise into features, each drawing an accuracy response. All three answer one kind of question: sweep a factor from tiny to huge — how do scores move? The x-axes are α (1→10), β (1→10), and noise ratio (0→80%).

例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（左格 (a)：α 拧不动成绩）：α 从 1 调到 10，红黑两线几乎水平——RD 在 75.5–76.7 之间起伏、DD 在 74.5–75.7 之间起伏，起落不到 1.5 个点。结论：α 不敏感。为什么？回看第 30 卡：α 在分子上、只是把所有位置分整体放大或缩小——后续打分机器能轻松吃掉这个统一缩放（第 30 卡迁移题的原话）。

Walk 1 (left panel (a): α cannot move the scores): sweeping α 1→10, both curves run almost flat — RD wobbles within 75.5–76.7, DD within 74.5–75.7, under a 1.5-point swing. Verdict: α-insensitive. Why? Card 30: α sits in the numerator, scaling all positions uniformly — a uniform scale the scoring machine absorbs easily (card 30's transfer answer).

走查 2（中格 (b)：β 一拧，红线跳水）：β 从 10 减到 1，红线（RD）明显下坠——β = 1 时它掉到五成上下；而黑线（DD）基本守住 76 一线。两线在低 β 区张开约 20–25 个点（正文说"约 10 个百分点"，图上按横轴刻度估算更大——这类"读图约值"与正文数不一致时，以你自己量的为准并记账，第 48 卡）。为什么 β 敏感？它在分母上，改变的是"衰减形状"（第 30 卡），深层规则的分量相对根层大变——而 DD 还带着结构账这一路，单路知识变差时另一路托底。

Walk 2 (middle panel (b): twist β, the red line dives): dropping β 10→1, red (RD) plunges — near five-tens at β = 1 — while black (DD) holds around 76. The two pull apart by roughly 20–25 points at low β (the text says "about 10 percentage points"; estimating from the axis the gap looks larger — when read-off values clash with the text, trust your own measurement and keep the account; card 48). Why β-sensitive? It sits in the denominator, reshaping the decay (card 30), so deep rules' relative weight shifts sharply — and DD still carries the structural stream to cushion a weakening partner.

走查 3（右格 (c)：噪声下的四条命）：噪声按 X̃ = (1−α)·X + α·ε 掺入（ε 是均值 0、波动 0.5 的正态噪声；α 这里指噪声比例，与 (a)(b) 的 α 同名不同物——记号冲突，论文的锅）。横轴 0→80% 时：DD 从 76 缓降到 71.5（最稳）；RD 从 74.5 降到 70；GBDT 72→65（缓降但仍跌）；蓝线 HGNN 崩得最快——它掉的具体点数，习题会请你上图量一次。为什么 HGNN 最脆？第 22 卡的软肋①——它靠"沿超边平均"吃饭，噪声被平均放大传播到邻居；而 GBDT 不平均、DD 有结构账托底，都比它抗噪。

Walk 3 (right panel (c): four lives under noise): noise enters as X̃ = (1−α)·X + α·ε (ε normal noise, mean 0, spread 0.5; here α is the noise ratio — same letter as (a)(b)'s α, a different beast: the paper's notation clash, not yours). Sweeping 0→80%: DD eases 76 → 71.5 (steadiest); RD falls 74.5 → 70; GBDT 72 → 65 (gentle but real); blue HGNN collapses fastest — the exercises will have you measure its drop off the chart. Why is HGNN frailest? Card 22's soft spot (1) — it lives on "averaging along hyperedges", and averaging broadcasts noise to neighbors; GBDT never averages and DD has the structural lifeline — both outlast it.
