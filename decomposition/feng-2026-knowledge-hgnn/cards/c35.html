是什么WHAT

这个面板把第 29–32 卡的四步缝成了一台可玩的机器：三个滑块给顶点设定特征 (x₁, x₂, x₃)，两个旋钮调位置公式 (α、β)，一个旋钮假装"结构分"（第 33 卡那个 Xs）。下面实时显示：哪些规则被满足、每条规则得到多少分、拼出来的 Xr 与融合账长什么样。

This panel stitches cards 29–32 into one playable machine: three sliders set the vertex's features (x₁, x₂, x₃), two knobs tune the position formula (α, β), and one knob fakes the "structural score" (card 33's Xs). Below it, live: which rules are satisfied, each rule's score, and the resulting Xr plus the fused ledger.

树队固定为第 27 卡那两棵：T₁ 问 x₂ ≥ 2（第 0 层）、x₁ ≥ 1（第 1 层）；T₂ 问 x₃ ≥ 5（第 0 层）、x₁ ≥ 2（第 1 层）、x₂ ≥ 1（第 1 层）。先预测再动手——每改一次滑块，先猜猜哪几格会变。

The team is fixed as card 27's two trees: T₁ asks x₂ ≥ 2 (layer 0), x₁ ≥ 1 (layer 1); T₂ asks x₃ ≥ 5 (layer 0), x₁ ≥ 2 (layer 1), x₂ ≥ 1 (layer 1). Predict before you touch — at each change, guess which slots will move.
