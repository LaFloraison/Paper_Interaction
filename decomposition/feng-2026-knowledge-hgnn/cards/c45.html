表 VIII：多叠几层，成绩会怎样Table VIII: what more layers do to the score

是什么WHAT

第 17 卡许过的愿在这里兑现：过平滑的代价到底多大？表 VIII 只拧一个旋钮——打分机器叠几层（2 / 4 / 6 / 8 层），看普通 HGNN、RD-HGNN、DD-HGNN 三列的准确率怎么塌。

Card 17's promise comes due here: how costly is over-smoothing, really? Table VIII twists one knob — how many layers the scoring machine stacks (2 / 4 / 6 / 8) — and watches plain HGNN, RD-HGNN and DD-HGNN collapse.

读数前记牢第 22 卡的机制：每多一层 = 多一轮"沿超边平均"。层数就是平均次数——平均越多，顶点特征越趋同（过平滑），个性越薄。所以这张表读的是"平均轮数的账"。

Recall card 22's mechanism before reading: every extra layer = one more round of "averaging along hyperedges". Layers are averaging rounds — more rounds homogenize vertices (over-smoothing), thinning their individuality. This table is the ledger of averaging rounds.

例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（普通 HGNN 列：一场雪崩）：逐行 73.41（2 层）→ 51.10（4 层）→ 43.19（6 层）→ 30.22（8 层）。落差 73.41 − 30.22 = 43.19——整整跌掉 43 个百分点！参照系：40 类任务随机瞎猜只有 2.5%，8 层时只剩 30.22，"个性"被平均掉了一大半。这张表第一次把"过平滑"从叙事场的灰色封面变成了一个可量化的数字。

Walk 1 (the plain HGNN column: an avalanche): row by row 73.41 (2 layers) → 51.10 (4) → 43.19 (6) → 30.22 (8). The plunge is 73.41 − 30.22 = 43.19 — a 43-point loss! Frame of reference: blind guessing on 40 classes scores 2.5%; at 8 layers only 30.22 remains — more than half the individuality averaged away. This table turns over-smoothing from the story's gray covers into a number.

走查 2（三列并排比"谁耐叠"）：DD：76.06 → 62.45 → 57.83 → 49.53（落差 76.06 − 49.53 = 26.53）；RD：74.39 → 50.95 → 39.50 → 40.39（RD 的落差是多少？下一题请你亲手算）。把三列放在一起看：DD 跌得最少（26.5），普通 HGNN 跌得最狠（43.2，走查 1 已算）。读法：层数加深时，"两路知识"里至少结构账不参与平均（第 24 卡），所以 DD 的底线最高；普通 HGNN 全靠平均撑着，一叠就散。顺带注意 RD 在 8 层反超了 6 层（40.39 > 39.50）——这条小波动说明"跌"不是一条直线，但大方向不变。

Walk 2 (three columns race for depth-endurance): DD: 76.06 → 62.45 → 57.83 → 49.53 (a drop of 76.06 − 49.53 = 26.53); RD: 74.39 → 50.95 → 39.50 → 40.39 (RD's drop? The next exercise asks you to compute it). Side by side: DD loses least (26.5), plain HGNN most (43.2, from walk 1). Reading: as depth grows, at least the structural ledger never joins the averaging (card 24), so DD keeps the highest floor; plain HGNN lives on averaging alone and crumbles with each stack. Note also RD at 8 layers edges its own 6-layer score (40.39 > 39.50) — the fall is not a clean line, yet the trend holds.
