是什么WHAT

表 IV 是复现实验的钥匙：每个数据集一行，记着训练用的全部可调项——学习率、树队的棵数、隐层维度、树的最大深度，以及位置公式的两个旋钮 α、β。表 I/II/III 描述"考什么、怎么切"，这张表描述"怎么练"。

Table IV is the reproducibility key: one row per dataset recording every tunable in training — learning rate, number of trees, hidden width, tree depth, and the position formula's two knobs α, β. Tables I–III describe what is examined and how it splits; this table describes how the model trains.

六列各管一处，其中三列你在方法篇已经亲自用过：α、β 就是第 30 卡位置公式的两只旋钮；树数决定规则账的长度（第 32 卡的 Cr）；最大深度决定每棵树的提问点上限（第 13 卡算过深度 4 → 15 个内部节点）。

Six columns, each minding one thing — three of which you have already handled in the method part: α, β are card 30's two knobs; number of trees sets the ledger's length (card 32's Cr); max depth caps each tree's question nodes (card 13 counted 15 inner nodes at depth 4).

例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（读 CC-Cora 行，接上方法篇）：学习率 0.4；树 140 棵；隐层 128 维；最大深度 3；α = 9、β = 8——正是第 30 卡例 1 用过的那组！回看那张小表：第 0 层权重 9÷8 = 1.125、第 2 层 9÷10 = 0.9——你当时算的数就是从这一行取的。这就是复现的意义：每个数字都能追到出处。

Walk 1 (read CC-Cora's row, joining the method part): learning rate 0.4; 140 trees; 128 hidden units; max depth 3; α = 9, β = 8 — card 30's Example 1 exactly! Look back at that little table: layer 0 weighs 9÷8 = 1.125, layer 2 weighs 0.9 — the numbers you computed came from this row. That is reproducibility: every figure traces to its source.

走查 2（竖着读两列，看规律）：树数列从 20（Tencent、Heloc）到 190（Amazon-P、Amazon-C、ModelNet40），跨度近十倍——数据集越大、特征越繁，配方里的树就越多。再看隐层维度一列：十行全是 128——这是全表唯一的"统一参数"，说明论文没有为每个数据集单独调它，保持了配置的简洁。最后看 α、β：每一行都不重样（10/6、9/8、6/6、6/10……）——这两只旋钮是逐数据集网格搜索出来的（第 30 卡提过），因为树的深度分布各数据集不同。

Walk 2 (read two columns vertically for patterns): the tree column runs from 20 (Tencent, Heloc) to 190 (Amazon-P, Amazon-C, ModelNet40), a tenfold span — bigger data, busier features, more trees. Now the hidden-width column: all ten rows read 128 — the table's only uniform setting, so the paper never hand-tuned it per dataset, keeping the recipe lean. Finally α, β: no two rows repeat (10/6, 9/8, 6/6, 6/10 …) — the two knobs came from per-dataset grid search (noted at card 30), because tree-depth profiles differ across datasets.
