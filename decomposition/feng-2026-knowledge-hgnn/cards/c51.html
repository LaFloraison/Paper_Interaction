① 流水线（一口气读完）(1) The pipeline in one breath

超图（点 + 超边）→ 两本账并行开账：HOI 数"参加几条超边"（式 3）／TDR 把 GBDT 树队的提问点收成规则账，逐条算"位置分 × 内容分"（式 4–8）→ MDK-Fusion：先 ⊙ 逐位相乘、再 ‖ 拼接成 Z（式 9）→ HGNN 顺着超图平均后分类（式 1）→ 两种装料方式得两个型号：RD（只喂规则账）、DD（喂 Z）（式 10）。

Hypergraph (vertices + hyperedges) → two ledgers opened in parallel: HOI counts memberships (Eq. 3) / TDR harvests GBDT question nodes into a rule ledger scoring "position × content" per rule (Eqs. 4–8) → MDK-Fusion: ⊙ multiply, then ‖ concatenate into Z (Eq. 9) → HGNN averages along the hypergraph, then classifies (Eq. 1) → two loading plans, two models: RD (rule ledger only), DD (the fused Z) (Eq. 10).

④ 带走这三句(4) Three sentences worth carrying out

1
知识的形状决定它的用法。结构知识是"计数"（一个数），规则知识是"对错清单"（一排数）——两种形状的账，合订时需要 ⊙ 这样的"渗入"而非简单拼接。Knowledge's shape dictates its use. Structural knowledge is a count (one number); rule knowledge is a checklist (a row) — fusing differently shaped ledgers needs ⊙-style seepage, not mere pasting.
2
抗丢信息的办法是"另开一页"。平均会抹平、蒸馏会压小、归一化会改写——论文的对策从不是"更小心地平均"，而是把易丢的东西单独存档：HOI 存结构、TDR 存规则。这个思路可以搬去任何"会丢东西的流水线"。The antidote to information loss is a separate page. Averaging flattens, distillation squeezes, normalization rewrites — and the paper never fights it with "more careful averaging" but by archiving what would be lost: HOI for structure, TDR for rules. The idea transplants to any lossy pipeline.
3
数字都要问口径。同一篇论文里，"Cora 提升"可以是 7.30 也可以是 6.83，"平均排名"可以是 1.7 也可以是 1.9——本站复核出的十笔账，每一笔都是口径与验算的课。Every number has a convention. Within one paper, "gain on Cora" is 7.30 or 6.83 and "average rank" is 1.7 or 1.9 — our ten ledger entries are ten lessons in conventions and verification.
