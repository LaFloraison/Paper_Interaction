Equation 3 · the accountant's formula公式 3 · 会计的公式

式 (3)：把"数人脉"写成一行字Eq. (3): "counting memberships" as one line

是什么WHAT

上一卡会计干的活——数参加几个组、压成 0–1 的一个数——论文用一行公式记了下来，这就是式 (3)。它短得可以背下来，但含两个我们还没讲透的零件。

The accountant's job from the last card — count groups, squash into a 0-to-1 number — is recorded in one line, the paper's Eq. (3). Short enough to memorize, yet it holds two parts we have not unpacked.

式 (3) 说的是：对每个顶点，先把它在编组表里那一行的 1 全加起来（=数它参加几个组），再把这个和塞进小函数 ρ 压成 0–1 的一个数。ρ 就是第 24 卡那个"压一压"的小函数，本卡末尾给它正式名分。

Eq. (3) says: for each vertex, first add up all the 1s in its row of the roster table (that is, count its groups), then feed that sum through the small function ρ to squash it into a 0-to-1 number. ρ is card 24's "squash" function, officially named at the end of this card.

第一个零件是那串求和记号——∑（念"西格玛"）=“把后面每一条都加起来”，这里是把某个顶点在各条超边下的取值全加一遍。第二个零件是 ρ 的具体长相：论文取最简的一层——ρ(x) = σ(w·x + b)，w、b 是两个可调旋钮（论文用 w=2、b=0），σ 仍是"压到 0–1"的那个函数。照旧：公式只是翻译，算全部由例 1 完成。

The first part is the summation sign — ∑ ("sigma") = "add up every term that follows", here summing one vertex's entries across hyperedges. The second part is ρ's concrete shape: the paper's simplest layer, ρ(x) = σ(w·x + b), with two tunable knobs w, b (the paper uses w = 2, b = 0) and σ still the "squash into 0–1" function. As always: the formula is translation only; Example 1 does the computing.

逐符号对照（读完抬头就走）：E = 全部超边的集合；H[v,e] = 编组表第 v 行第 e 列的数（0 或 1）；∑e∈E = 把 v 在所有超边下的这些 0/1 加起来 = v 参加的超边条数；ρ = "压一压"的小函数；w、b = 两个可调旋钮；σ = 把任意数压到 0–1。整式一句话：存档第 v 个数 = 压一压（v 的参加条数）。Symbol by symbol (read and walk away): E = the set of all hyperedges; H[v,e] = entry (0 or 1) at row v, column e of the roster; ∑e∈E = adding all those 0/1s = how many hyperedges v joins; ρ = the squash function; w, b = two tunable knobs; σ = squashing anything into 0–1. The whole line in one breath: archive[v] = squash(count of v's memberships).

最后一个新名词：ρ 被论文限制为"逐顶点"作用——它每次只吃一个数（那个参加条数），对其他顶点、对超边顺序一无所知。这个限制不是随手加的，它是下一卡"列交换不变"证明的另一半地基。

One last term: ρ is restricted to act vertex-wise — it eats one number at a time (that membership count) and knows nothing about other vertices or about edge order. Not a casual restriction: it is the other foundation stone of the next card's column-exchange proof.

例子EXAMPLE

例 1（5 顶点新沙盘，跟着算）：新编组表：v1 参加 3 个组、v2 参加 1 个、v3 参加 2 个、v4 参加 2 个、v5 谁也不参加（0 个，孤立顶点）。用 ρ(x) = σ(2x)，σ 的算法仍是 1 ÷ (1 + e−x)：
v1 → σ(6) = 1 ÷ (1 + 0.0025) ≈ 0.9975；v2 → σ(2) ≈ 0.8808；v3、v4 → σ(4) ≈ 0.9820；v5 → σ(0) = 1 ÷ (1 + 1) = 0.5。存档 = [0.9975, 0.8808, 0.9820, 0.9820, 0.5]。

Example 1 (a fresh 5-vertex sandbox, compute along): new roster: v1 joins 3 groups, v2 joins 1, v3 joins 2, v4 joins 2, v5 joins none (0 — an isolated vertex). With ρ(x) = σ(2x) and σ(x) = 1 ÷ (1 + e−x):
v1 → σ(6) = 1 ÷ (1 + 0.0025) ≈ 0.9975; v2 → σ(2) ≈ 0.8808; v3, v4 → σ(4) ≈ 0.9820; v5 → σ(0) = 1 ÷ (1 + 1) = 0.5. Archive = [0.9975, 0.8808, 0.9820, 0.9820, 0.5].

例 2（孤立顶点的 0.5 值得读两遍）：v5 谁都不挨着，参加 0 个组。按式 (3)，它照样有一份存档，值是 0.5——不偏不倚的正中。这个数字的含义：“关于它的结构，什么都不知道”。老方法的麻烦恰在这里：孤立顶点在平均里没有任何声音；而式 (3) 给它的 0.5 是一份明明白白的"无信息"声明，后续融合时可以被公平处理。

Example 2 (the isolated vertex's 0.5 deserves two reads): v5 touches nobody and joins 0 groups. By Eq. (3) it still gets an archive — value 0.5, dead center, meaning "nothing is known about its structure". This is exactly where the old method struggles: an isolated vertex has no voice in averaging; Eq. (3)'s 0.5 is an explicit declaration of "no information", which later fusion can handle fairly.
