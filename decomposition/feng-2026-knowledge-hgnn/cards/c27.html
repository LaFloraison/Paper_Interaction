TDR-Encoder：把树的"提问点"收成规则账TDR-Encoder: turning a tree's questions into a rule ledger

是什么WHAT

备料车间的第二条流水线，是查规则的学究。他手里有一队已经训练好的树（第 12、13 卡的"接力纠错树队"：每棵树都在补前一棵的漏）。但学究不拿树去预测——他只要每棵树在分叉处问过的那些问题。

The prep floor's second line is the rule-reading scholar. In his hands sits a team of trained trees (cards 12–13's "relay of error-correcting trees": each patching the previous one's leaks). Yet the scholar never predicts with them — he only wants the questions asked at each branching point.

TDR-Encoder 做的是：把树队里每一个"提问点"收成一条规则；每条规则配两条记录——① 内容（问的是什么，如"x₂ ≥ 2 吗"）；② 位置（在第几层问的）。论文给这两条记录各起了一个代号：内容映射叫 μc，位置映射叫 μp（正式公式在第 29、30 卡，本卡只需认得这两个代号）。

The TDR-Encoder collects every question node in the tree team into a rule; each rule carries two records — (1) content (what is asked, e.g. "is x₂ ≥ 2") and (2) position (which layer asked it). The paper codenames the two records: μc for content mapping, μp for position mapping (equations at cards 29–30; here you only need the codenames).

为什么不要树的预测？因为预测是"当时那个任务"的答案——换个任务立刻作废；而提问点本身是特征与任务之间的规律，规律比答案长寿（第 11 卡的老道理）。所以学究的账本里只抄问题、不抄结论。

Why skip the tree's predictions? Predictions are the answers to "that old task" — void the moment the task changes; the questions themselves are patterns linking features to the task, and patterns outlive answers (card 11's old lesson). Hence the ledger copies questions only, never conclusions.

账本最终长什么样？对每个顶点、每条规则，会算出一个数——它由"内容对不对"和"位置权重"两部分相乘得来。这个"相乘"是下一组卡的主角；本卡先把"账本里有哪些条目"数清楚。

What does the finished ledger look like? For every vertex and every rule, one number is computed — the product of "is the content satisfied" and "the position weight". That product stars in the coming cards; this card first counts the ledger's entries.

常见误解：Common misconception: 别以为 TDR 是"换一棵更好的树来做预测"——树在本文里从头到尾只当规则仓库用，预测结果一律丢掉。真正干活的是规则账，不是树。 Do not think the TDR swaps in a "better tree to predict with" — the trees here serve as a rule warehouse only; predictions are all discarded. The ledger works; the trees just store.
例子EXAMPLE

例 1（从树队收规则，数条目）：设树队只有两棵树（论文图 2 画的那两棵）：T₁ 有两个提问点：根问"x₂ ≥ 2？"，下层问"x₁ ≥ 1？"；T₂ 有三个："x₃ ≥ 5？"、"x₁ ≥ 2？"、"x₂ ≥ 1？"。学究逐棵抄录，账本共 2 + 3 = 5 条规则。叶子上的 A / B / C 标签一律不抄。

Example 1 (collect rules, count entries): suppose the team holds just the two trees drawn in the paper's Fig. 2: T₁ asks two questions — root "x₂ ≥ 2?" and lower "x₁ ≥ 1?"; T₂ asks three — "x₃ ≥ 5?", "x₁ ≥ 2?", "x₂ ≥ 1?". The scholar copies tree by tree: 2 + 3 = 5 rules. Leaf labels A / B / C are never copied.

例 2（两个顶点，两份不同的清单）：把"内容对不对"先记成 对/不对（第 29 卡会把"对"记作 0、"不对"记作 1）。顶点甲的数值 x = (1, 3, 4)：x₂ ≥ 2？3 ≥ 2 对；x₁ ≥ 1？1 ≥ 1 对；x₃ ≥ 5？4 ≥ 5 不对；x₁ ≥ 2？1 ≥ 2 不对；x₂ ≥ 1？3 ≥ 1 对——清单 = 对、对、不对、不对、对。顶点乙 x = (3, 3, 1)：对、对、不对、对、对。同一批 5 条规则，两个顶点各得一份自己的对错清单——账本按人开页。

Example 2 (two vertices, two different lists): record content as satisfied/not (card 29 writes "satisfied" as 0, "not" as 1). Vertex A's values x = (1, 3, 4): x₂ ≥ 2? 3 ≥ 2 yes; x₁ ≥ 1? 1 ≥ 1 yes; x₃ ≥ 5? 4 ≥ 5 no; x₁ ≥ 2? 1 ≥ 2 no; x₂ ≥ 1? 3 ≥ 1 yes — list = yes, yes, no, no, yes. Vertex B x = (3, 3, 1): yes, yes, no, yes, yes. Same five rules, each vertex gets its own satisfied/missed list — the ledger keeps a page per person.
