表 VII 只变一个量：树队的棵数（5、10、50、100、150、200），看它如何影响 GBDT 基线与本文两个型号的准确率——回答"规则账该多长"。数据集固定为 CC-Cora（消融实验的常驻考场），六行 × 三列，加粗标每行最优。

Table VII varies one quantity only: the number of trees (5, 10, 50, 100, 150, 200), tracking its effect on the GBDT baseline and the two models — answering "how long should the rule ledger be". The dataset is CC-Cora (the ablation's standing venue); six rows × three columns, bold marks each row's best.

读它之前回想两条既有知识：树越多 = 提问点越多 = 规则账越长（第 32 卡的 Cr）；而"树太多会过拟合"在第 10、12 卡都埋过伏笔——这张表就是把伏笔挖出来验收。

Two prior lessons to recall: more trees = more question nodes = a longer rule ledger (card 32's Cr); and "too many trees overfit" was seeded back in cards 10 and 12 — this table excavates those seeds.
