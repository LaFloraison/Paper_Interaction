是什么WHAT

表 VI 是实务卷（production）的成绩单：每个数据集占三行——Production（直推+归纳合并）、Transductive、Inductive；后两列 Δ 记的是"本文最好的型号减基线"的增益。它比表 V 多回答一个问题：见过题的点和全新点，成绩差多少？

Table VI is the realistic exam's score sheet: three lines per dataset — Production (transductive + inductive merged), Transductive, Inductive; the two Δ columns record "our best model minus baseline". Beyond Table V it answers one more question: how far apart are scores on seen points versus brand-new ones?

读 Δ 列前先约定口径（我们逐格验过）：Δ 列 = max(RD, DD) − 基线——即"两个型号里较好的那个"与基线的差。自检一例即可上手：Heloc 的 Production 行，RD 72.55、DD 72.27 → 取大者 72.55；GBDT 基线 69.90 → 72.55 − 69.90 = 2.65，与表里印的 +2.65 一致。

A convention before the Δ columns (we verified it cell by cell): Δ = max(RD, DD) − baseline — the better of the two models against the baseline. One self-check to get going: Heloc's Production line, RD 72.55, DD 72.27 → take 72.55; GBDT baseline 69.90 → 72.55 − 69.90 = 2.65, matching the printed +2.65.

例子EXAMPLE

走查 1（Cora 三行，连读）：Production：RD 88.25、HGNN 81.91 → ΔHGNN = 6.34；Transductive：RD 88.89、HGNN 81.59 → ΔHGNN = 7.30；Inductive：RD 87.53、HGNN 83.21 → ΔHGNN = 4.32。三个观察：① 同一模型直推行最好、归纳行最差（见过 vs 没见过）；② 7.30 这个数就是摘要里"7.3% gain on Cora"的出处（transductive 行、RD 对 HGNN）——复算 88.89 − 81.59 = 7.30 ✓，但注意它与表 V 的 Cora 格（88.64 / 81.81）不同：同一数据集在不同表里数字略有出入（分表统计与四舍五入所致）；③ 归纳行虽低，仍高基线 4.32——新点也没白学。

Walk 1 (Cora's three lines, read in sequence): Production: RD 88.25, HGNN 81.91 → ΔHGNN = 6.34; Transductive: RD 88.89, HGNN 81.59 → ΔHGNN = 7.30; Inductive: RD 87.53, HGNN 83.21 → ΔHGNN = 4.32. Three observations: (1) the same model peaks on transductive and dips on inductive (seen versus unseen); (2) 7.30 is the source of the abstract's "7.3% gain on Cora" (transductive line, RD vs HGNN) — recompute 88.89 − 81.59 = 7.30 ✓ — yet note it differs from Table V's Cora cells (88.64 / 81.81): the same dataset carries slightly different numbers across tables (per-table statistics and rounding); (3) even the inductive line stays 4.32 above baseline — unseen points are not wasted.

走查 2（两处"表自己的账"）：① 表格底部的"平均差"一栏印着 ΔGBDT 均值 +6.31、ΔHGNN +2.53；我们把 Production 十行复算：ΔGBDT 均值 5.94、ΔHGNN 均值 2.72——两个都不完全对得上（差 0.2–0.4）。② Github 的 Production 行 ΔHGNN 印着 +0.00，而该行数值是 HGNN 86.05、DD 85.96：85.96 − 86.05 = −0.09——本文型号其实略输，"0.00"是四舍五入后的呈现。结论：这张表的正文与表格都有几笔对不平的账，读数前先自己验一遍是最稳的姿势（第 48 批判卡汇总）。

Walk 2 (two accounts the table keeps with itself): (1) the footer's average-gap row prints ΔGBDT mean +6.31, ΔHGNN +2.53; we recomputed the ten Production lines: ΔGBDT mean 5.94, ΔHGNN mean 2.72 — neither matches exactly (off by 0.2–0.4). (2) Github's Production line prints ΔHGNN +0.00, yet the line reads HGNN 86.05, DD 85.96: 85.96 − 86.05 = −0.09 — the paper's model actually trails slightly, and "0.00" is the rounded rendering. Verdict: several entries in this table and its text do not balance; verifying before reading is the safest posture (card 48 gathers them).
