顺带结清：与最像的前人工作 BGNN 的界线Settling the account: the line against BGNN, the nearest predecessor

论文主动区分了与 BGNN（Boost-then-Convolve，2020，本文表 V 里 TLF 的同族）的差别：BGNN 把 GBDT 当预处理器——把异质的表格特征翻译成同质的表示后交给图网络，GBDT 学到的规则本身并没有与结构信息融合；本文的 TDR-Encoder 显式编码规则的内容与位置，再经 MDK-Fusion 与结构账深度融合。

The paper draws its line against BGNN (Boost-then-Convolve, 2020 — kin of Table V's TLF): BGNN uses GBDT as a preprocessor, translating heterogeneous tabular features into a homogeneous representation for the graph network, without ever fusing the learned rules with structure; this paper's TDR-Encoder encodes each rule's content and position and deeply fuses them with the structural ledger via MDK-Fusion.

一句话分辨：BGNN 用树的"输出"，本文用树的"问题"。前者是流水线拼接，后者是深度耦合——这是论文在相关工作里给自己划的核心边界。

One line to tell them apart: BGNN uses the tree's "outputs", this paper the tree's "questions". Pipeline plumbing versus deep coupling — the boundary the paper draws for itself.
